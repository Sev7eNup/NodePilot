# Code of Conduct

## Pledge

NodePilot is a small, open project. Everyone who takes part in it, whether by reporting a bug, opening a pull request, asking a question or answering one, is entitled to be treated with respect. This applies regardless of experience level, background, identity or the language a person is most comfortable in.

## Expected behaviour

- **Be direct and be kind.** Technical disagreement is welcome and useful, contempt is not. Criticism is aimed at the code, the design or the argument and never at the person who brought it.
- **Assume competence and good faith.** Should a question look obvious, this usually means that the documentation failed and not the person asking.
- **Write for people who do not share your context.** A statement needs enough context that someone else can reproduce it, verify it or disagree with it.
- **English is the working language on GitHub**, which covers issues, pull requests and reviews. What matters is whether the point comes across, not the grammar. Imperfect English is entirely fine, while correcting someone's grammar unasked is not.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks, in public as well as in private.
- Discriminatory language or imagery, including sexualised language, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or deliberately derailing threads.
- Encouraging or excusing any of the above.

## Security reports are separate

A vulnerability is **not** reported in a public issue. The private reporting path is described in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md). Reporting a security problem in good faith is always welcome, even when it turns out to be mistaken, and will never be held against you.

## Scope

This document applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, code review and the project's documentation. It also applies when someone represents the project in public elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Anything that concerns you can be reported to **florian.kemper.ms@gmail.com**. Reports are handled confidentially, and every report receives an acknowledgement.

Since this is a one-maintainer project, there is no committee and no appeals board, and the decision lies with the maintainer. Depending on severity, the response ranges from a private word through editing or removing the offending content to blocking an account from the repository. The person concerned is told the reasoning.

The maintainer is not exempt from this document.
